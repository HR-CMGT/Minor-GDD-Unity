# Week 1.8: Blocktober Demo & Midterm Milestone Review

This session is dedicated to **Blocktober Demo playtesting** and code review of 3D block prototypes.

---

## 🎯 Milestone Objectives
1. Conduct peer playtests on the 3D ProBuilder greybox levels created in [Lesson 1.7](07_blockprototyping.md).
2. Validate player movement speed, jump height, camera framing, and corridor clearances.
3. Incorporate code and design feedback before advancing to [Lesson 1.9: Debugging & Profiling](09_problemsolving.md).

---

## 📋 Playtest Review Checklist

- [ ] **Level Metrics:** Are doorways ($>1.2\text{ m}$) and corridors ($>3.0\text{ m}$) wide enough for camera clearance?
- [ ] **Movement Responsiveness:** Do jumping and landing feel tight without snagging on edges?
- [ ] **Framerate & Performance:** Does the game maintain a rock-solid 60+ FPS in Game View?
- [ ] **Code Separation:** Are visual renderers on child objects and physics on the root of prefabs?
